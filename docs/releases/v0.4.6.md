## Summary

This candidate upgrades the Agent Lens and Weave Agents toolsets to the official
`v1` tag APIs and full Insights, cluster/volume, stratified sampling,
instrumentation-doctor, guideline, grouped-span, and human-feedback primitive
set, hardens ARIA turn polling and root-thread titling, adds service-account
viewer identity attribution and stateless telemetry safeguards, and updates
security/runtime dependencies (`wandb>=0.31.2`, `cryptography>=50.0.2`,
`certifi>=2026.11.20`). No deployment channel is available yet.

## Changes

- **Interactive W&B Line Plot MCP App (`models` group):**
  - Adds `render_wandb_line_plot_tool` and self-contained offline `ui://wandb/line-plot.html` (`@modelcontextprotocol/ext-apps` + `uPlot`, MIT-licensed) supporting 1-call auto-discovery (`entity_name` and `run_ids` optional, `metric` shorthand) as well as full camelCase `RunsLinePlotConfig` panels, fullscreen/PiP expansion, outward `_step` drag-zoom, client-side smoothing/log-Y/isolate controls, 10-second active-run live refresh, and stateless `ui/update-model-context` synchronization.
- **Agent Lens official `v1` and Insights/Guidelines primitives (`agent-lens` extension):**
  - Migrates `list_agent_lens_tags_tool` to `GET /api/v1/tags` (with single-tag `tag_id` lookup) and adds `manage_agent_lens_tags_tool` (`read-write`) for `v1` tag CRUD (`POST /api/v1/tags`, `POST/DELETE /api/v1/tags/{id}`) plus human turn tag assignment/removal (`POST/DELETE /api/conversation-tags`).
  - Migrates `get_agent_lens_category_breakdowns_tool` from the retired `/insights/intent-category-breakdowns` route to live `GET /api/insights/{type}/breakdowns` (`signature_type="both"` by default, turn-level `filters`, and flat `top_n_clusters` ranking).
  - Adds `list_agent_lens_clusters_tool` (`GET /api/insights/clusters` plus optional `GET /api/insights/{type}/volume` bucketed series), stratified multi-cluster sampling via `per_topic_limit` on `list_agent_lens_category_example_turns_tool`, paginated turn-level tag assignments (`GET /api/tags/{id}/assignments`) on `list_agent_lens_tagged_conversations_tool`, `selected_failure` pinning on `get_agent_lens_failure_attributions_tool`, `get_agent_lens_guidelines_tool` + `manage_agent_lens_guidelines_tool` for project judge rules and conversation summarization, and `diagnose_agent_lens_instrumentation_tool` for ranked 16-rule OTel GenAI tracing diagnostics.
- **Weave Agents grouped spans, chronological chat, and human feedback (`agents` group):**
  - Extends `query_weave_agent_spans_tool` and `get_weave_agent_span_stats_tool` with `group_by` (including custom attributes), `measures`, `percentiles`, `insight_filters`, `tag_filter`, and `signal_filters`; defaults `get_weave_agent_conversation_tool` to chronological `sort_direction="asc"` (`Turn 1` first) with oversized tool-output compaction before turn truncation; and adds `get_weave_agent_conversation_spans_tool`, `query_weave_agent_feedback_tool`, and `manage_weave_agent_feedback_tool` (`read-write`) for batch span trees and project-wide free-text human notes (`wandb.note.1`) and reactions (`wandb.agent_user_feedback`).
- **ARIA polling and UX hardening (`aria` extension):**
  - Stepped bounded polling (`1s`, `2s`, `3s` cap) with confirmed-snapshot preservation on transient mid-poll failures (`progress.poll_interrupted`), `call_id` tool-name correlation, optional root-turn `title` auto-generation, and bounded `include_turn=true` tail compaction.
- **Telemetry attribution and dependency floors:**
  - Caches structured `ViewerIdentity` across stateless requests with service-account/team attribution, suppresses unauthenticated Segment events while preserving Datadog operational counters, and bumps `wandb` to `0.31.2`, `cryptography` to `50.0.2` (retiring `GO-2026-6860`), and `certifi` to `2026.11.20`.

This release retains the orthogonal selector architecture from
[v0.4.5](v0.4.5.md) while expanding `agents` (10 read, 1 write) and
`agent-lens` (12 read, 2 write) within the 128 KiB `tools/list` budget.

## Compatibility

Python 3.11 and 3.12 and the MCP/W&B SDK ranges remain compatible with v0.4.5
(with minimum floors raised to `wandb>=0.31.2`, `cryptography>=50.0.2`, and
`certifi>=2026.11.20`). Existing tool names in `models`, `weave`, `agents`,
`agent-lens`, and `aria` are preserved; new write tools are omitted automatically
when `WANDB_MCP_ACCESS_MODE=read-only`.

## Breaking changes and migration

`get_agent_lens_category_breakdowns_tool` now queries live
`GET /api/insights/{type}/breakdowns` (returning `turn_count` and `categories`
for `intent`, `failure`, or `both` by default) instead of the retired
`/api/insights/intent-category-breakdowns` endpoint. All other changes are
backward-compatible extensions.
